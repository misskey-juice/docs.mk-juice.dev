# Antenna

"Notes from followed users" can be chosen as an antenna source (marked with a JUICE badge).

## Notes from followed users

- Picks up only notes from users that the antenna's owner follows.
- Your own notes and notes from people you do not follow are not picked up.
- It can be combined with other conditions such as keywords. For example, you can collect only the notes from people you follow that contain certain words.

::: info
In upstream Misskey, this source was removed from the options and was never actually implemented (choosing it behaved the same as "All notes"). On Juice Server, it works as its name says.
:::
