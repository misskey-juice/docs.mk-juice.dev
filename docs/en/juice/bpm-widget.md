# BPM counter widget

A widget for measuring tempo (BPM). Add it by choosing "BPM counter" when adding a widget.

## How to measure

- **Tap to measure**: press the button along to a rhythm, and the BPM is calculated from the last 16 intervals.
- **Measure from timelines or notifications**: shows how fast posts arrive on a timeline (Home, Local, Social, Global, Media, Relay) or notifications (the number in the last minute) as a BPM.

## Metronome

You can play a metronome at the measured BPM. The sound can be chosen, and the metronome can be turned on and off from the widget.
